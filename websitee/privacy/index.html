Legal & Data Transparency

Privacy
Policy.

This privacy policy explains how personal information is collected, used, and protected when you visit swarchougule.in or book a project consultation.

1. Information Collected

This website is primarily an informational portfolio. We do not require visitors to register an account or provide payment information directly on this website. The information collected is limited to:

  • Consultation Details: When you schedule a call via the Cal.com embed or link, you may provide your name, email address, company name, and brief notes regarding your project.
  • Standard Server Logs: Standard web server logs (such as IP addresses, browser user-agents, and timestamp data) collected by our hosting provider for security and operational monitoring.

2. How Information is Used

Any information you provide is used strictly to:

  • Respond to your direct inquiries and communicate regarding potential web design, development, or AI tool projects.
  • Facilitate the scheduling and conduction of introductory video or audio meetings.
  • Deliver relevant project proposals or estimates that you specifically request.

Your personal information is never sold, rented, or distributed to third-party marketing companies.

3. Third-Party Services

To deliver this website and its features, we utilize trusted third-party providers:

  • Cal.com: Used to provide live appointment scheduling and calendar management. Cal.com operates under its own privacy policy when processing booking data.
  • Google Fonts: Typography assets are served via Google Fonts to maintain visual consistency.
  • Hostinger: Provides web server infrastructure, SSL encryption, and static asset distribution.

4. Data Retention & Rights

Contact details submitted for project inquiries are retained only for as long as necessary to maintain client communications. If you would like your contact information deleted from past email or booking records, you may request deletion at any time by connecting via LinkedIn or responding to your meeting confirmation.

5. Policy Updates

This privacy policy is effective as of 2026. Any future adjustments will be published directly to this page with an updated modification notice.
