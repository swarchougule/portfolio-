Client Engagement & Workflow

How We Work
Together.

A structured, collaborative development process designed to eliminate confusion, prevent scope creep, and deliver high-impact digital products on time. Every phase provides tangible deliverables and direct check-ins.

Book discovery call ↗ Review the 6 stages ↓
01
Stage 01

Discovery & Strategy

Scope Document & Alignment

We begin with a focused discovery call to define your commercial goals, target audience, technical requirements, and core brand positioning. We clarify what success looks like and determine the optimal technical stack (custom code, Framer, WordPress, or Supabase).

02
Stage 02

Architecture & Design Direction

Wireframes & Visual Language

I establish the information architecture, sitemap, content flow, and typographic direction. We review high-fidelity prototypes to align on aesthetics, layout proportions, and interactive states before writing extensive production code.

03
Stage 03

Agile Development & Integration

Working Staging Environment

I engineer the frontend interface with clean, semantic markup and configure backends (database schemas on Supabase, auth states, or CMS collections). You receive access to a private staging URL to test progress in real time.

04
Stage 04

Testing, SEO & Performance

Audit & Optimization Pass

Rigorous cross-device testing across smartphones, tablets, and desktop browsers. We audit Core Web Vitals, implement structured data (JSON-LD), configure OpenGraph metadata, and verify keyboard accessibility.

05
Stage 05

Launch & Deployment

Production Release & Handover

Connecting your custom domain, setting up SSL certificates, generating XML sitemaps, submitting to search consoles, and conducting a final deployment checklist. I provide a walkthrough session so you feel completely confident managing your new site.

06
Stage 06

Iteration & Evolution

Ongoing Support & Feature Expansion

After launch, we monitor user engagement and real-world performance. As your business expands, we can add new service pages, introduce web app features, or integrate AI tools as your roadmap dictates.

02Workflow FAQs

What to Expect.

How much time will I need to dedicate during the build?

Most of the heavy lifting is handled directly by me. Your primary involvement happens during initial discovery, reviewing the design direction, providing brand copy or imagery, and final approval before launch.

How do we communicate throughout the project?

We stay connected via scheduled video check-ins and direct messaging (or email). You always have access to a live staging link to see exactly how development is advancing.

What happens after the website goes live?

Every launch includes post-launch support to resolve any unexpected issues and ensure your analytics, search indexing, and contact flows are functioning flawlessly.
