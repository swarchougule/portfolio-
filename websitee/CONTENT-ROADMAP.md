## 1. Executive Strategy & Content Funnel

### 1.1 Commercial Conversion Architecture
The content strategy exists to convert high-intent organic visitors into qualified enquiries via a structured 5-step funnel:

```
Step 1: High-Intent Search Query (Google)
       ↓
Step 2: Practical, Jargon-Free Guide (/blog/...)
       ↓
Step 3: Commercial Service Page (/services/web-design/ or /services/)
       ↓
Step 4: Tangible Proof & Case Studies (/work/hansraj-solutions/ or /work/)
       ↓
Step 5: Direct Action / Intro Call (/contact/ or Cal.com)
```

### 1.2 Target Audience Profile
- **Primary Audience:** Small and medium business owners, founders, local service providers, consultants, and B2B operators.
- **Pain Points:** 
  - Having an outdated website or relying exclusively on Instagram/social media.
  - Getting visitors but zero enquiries or phone calls.
  - Confusion over technical jargon, pricing variations, and website builders.
  - Running Meta ads that burn money because traffic hits an unoptimized homepage.
  - Frustration with slow, unreliable freelance developers or agencies with hidden fees.
- **Tone & Style Rules:**
  - Simple, direct, plain English.
  - Short paragraphs and scannable bullet points.
  - No AI cliché openings ("In today's digital landscape...").
  - Practical decision frameworks and realistic guidance without invented figures or fake claims.

## 6. Internal Linking & Anchor Text Strategy

To build durable topical authority without triggering algorithmic over-optimization penalties:
1. **Always use descriptive, conversational anchor texts:**
   - *Good:* "our [website design and development services](/services/web-design/)"
   - *Good:* "see how we structured the [Hansraj Solutions case study](/work/hansraj-solutions/)"
   - *Bad:* "click here", "read more", "best website designer in india"
2. **Cluster Hub Linkage:**
   - Every website design guide links back to the parent hub `/services/web-design/`.
   - Every Meta Ads guide links to the Landing Page / Conversion section and to `/contact/`.
   - All guides feature a clean author box linking to `/about/` and `/process/`.
3. **Cross-Article Contextual Links:**
   - *How to Make a Website* links to *Framer vs WordPress* when discussing platforms.
   - *How Can a Website Help My Business* links to *Why Your Ads Need a Landing Page* when discussing paid traffic.
   - *Website Development Process* links to *How Much Does a Website Cost* when outlining project scopes.

## 7. Content Quality Verification Checklist

Before publishing any future article from this roadmap, ensure it passes this strict QA checklist:
- [ ] Written in plain, conversational English that a busy non-technical business owner understands in 3 minutes.
- [ ] No buzzwords, empty corporate hype, or AI-generated filler sentences.
- [ ] Exactly one `<h1>` per page matching the page's core search focus.
- [ ] Unique title tag (< 60 chars) and unique meta description (< 155 chars).
- [ ] Includes at least one clear, real-world comparison table or scannable checklist.
- [ ] Integrates a natural, unforced link to `/services/web-design/` or relevant proof piece.
- [ ] Contains a respectful, low-pressure CTA pointing to a 15-minute consultation at `/contact/`.
- [ ] Includes valid `BlogPosting` and `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD schema referencing `#person` and `#website`.
- [ ] Added to `sitemap.xml` only after the page is verified live on the local HTTP server.
