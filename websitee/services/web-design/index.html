05Frequently Asked Questions

Website Design FAQs.

How long does a website project usually take?

A focused landing page or small business website typically takes 2 to 3 weeks from initial discovery to launch. Larger multi-page websites or e-commerce storefronts generally require 3 to 5 weeks depending on content readiness.

Should I choose Framer, WordPress, or custom code?

If you want rapid visual polish and easy visual editing, Framer is fantastic. If you need extensive blogging and dynamic database features, WordPress is a proven standard. If you want unmatched loading speed and zero platform dependency, lightweight custom code is best. We discuss this in our initial discovery call.

Do you design landing pages for Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)?

Yes. I design high-converting landing pages tailored specifically for Meta Ads campaigns, built mobile-first with clear value propositions, fast loading speeds, and friction-free inquiry funnels to maximize advertising ROI.

Can you redesign an existing website without losing search rankings?

Yes. We audit your existing site structure, retain critical URL pathways and metadata, and upgrade the visual identity, speed, and mobile responsiveness without damaging current Google search rankings.

Will my website be fast on mobile?

Yes. Mobile responsiveness and performance are foundational requirements, not afterthoughts. All image assets are delivered in next-gen formats (WebP), scripts are deferred, and layouts are tested across actual screen sizes.

Do you handle technical SEO and Google indexing?

Yes. Every page is structured with semantic HTML5, accurate meta titles and descriptions, canonical tags, OpenGraph previews, XML sitemaps, and robots.txt so search engines can crawl and index your content smoothly.
