Frequently Asked Questions.

Common questions business owners ask before scheduling an introductory conversation or sending an inquiry.

What should I prepare before our intro call?

Nothing formal is required. It helps to have a rough idea of your business goals, any existing website URL, examples of websites you like, and your approximate timeline.

How quickly can we start working together?

Once we agree on project scope, deliverables, and timeline, projects typically kick off within 3 to 7 business days.

Do you work with clients remotely across India and internationally?

Yes. I work remotely with clients throughout India, the US, UK, and worldwide. All communication happens seamlessly via Google Meet, Cal.com, WhatsApp, and email.

What happens after I submit this inquiry form?

I personally review your project details and respond via email or WhatsApp within 24 hours with initial thoughts, availability, and next steps.
