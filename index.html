Why Businesses Need a
Professional Website.

In 2026, prospective customers look up your website before deciding to call, message, or purchase. A slow, outdated, or confusing website loses leads immediately, while a fast, credibility-driven website works as an automated 24/7 sales asset for your business.

01 / Trust & Authority

Better Credibility

First impressions happen in under three seconds. A clean, custom website builds instant legitimacy and proves your business is established, dependable, and serious about quality.

02 / Lead Generation

More Enquiries

A business website should actively generate revenue. Conversion-focused layouts, clear call-to-action buttons, and frictionless forms turn casual visitors into genuine incoming client enquiries.

03 / Search Discoverability

Better Google Visibility

06Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions.

Clear answers to common questions business owners ask before hiring a website designer and developer. Have another question? Feel free to reach out directly.

How much does a business website cost?

The cost of a business website depends on the project scope, number of pages, and specific features needed. A single high-converting landing page has a different scope than a complete multi-page business website with case studies and custom enquiry funnels. Every project includes clear, upfront pricing with no hidden charges, tailored to small businesses and growing brands.

How long does website development take?

Most small business websites are designed, developed, and launched within 1 to 3 weeks. A focused landing page can often be completed in 5 to 7 days. Timelines depend primarily on how quickly content and feedback are provided during the review stages.

Do small businesses need a website?

Yes. While social media channels are useful for top-of-funnel reach, a dedicated website is the only digital asset your business completely owns. Potential clients check your website to verify credibility, read comprehensive service details, and get in touch without algorithm distractions or platform limitations.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. I regularly redesign outdated, slow, or template-bloated websites. A redesign modernizes your visual branding, improves mobile usability, accelerates page loading speeds, and fixes conversion drop-offs while carefully preserving your existing search engine rankings and URLs.

Which is better, Framer or WordPress?

Both platforms are powerful in different scenarios. Framer is ideal for modern, visual-first business websites and high-converting landing pages that need lightning-fast speed, smooth animations, and easy visual editing. WordPress is best suited for content-heavy sites with hundreds of articles, complex blogs, or advanced custom database setups. I help you choose the right platform based on your specific business goals.

Do you build landing pages for Meta Ads?

Yes. I build custom, high-converting landing pages specifically designed for Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram campaigns). These pages are built mobile-first, load in under two seconds, match your advertising messaging, and guide users directly toward submitting an enquiry or booking a consultation.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes, every website is developed mobile-first. More than 70% of commercial web traffic now comes from smartphones. I test extensively across iOS and Android screen sizes to ensure typography, navigation, touch targets, and loading speeds provide a seamless mobile experience.

How do I get started?

Getting started is simple. You can book a free 15-minute intro call or send a quick project message through the contact page. We will discuss your business goals, identify what type of website or landing page you need, and follow up with a clear proposal, timeline, and deliverables.
